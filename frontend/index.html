A URL or a claim isn't evidence by itself — evidence is what's found when a source is checked against original material, dates and other sources.

How a check is assessed

Every investigation ends in one of six outcomes. RealityCheck does not claim certainty it doesn't have.

Verified
Strong evidence supports the claim.
Partially verified
Some parts are supported while others remain uncertain.
Contradicted
Reliable evidence conflicts with the claim.
Insufficient evidence
There isn't enough reliable evidence to reach a conclusion.
Unresolved
Available evidence conflicts with itself, so a responsible conclusion can't be reached either way.
Potential manipulation
Technical or contextual indicators suggest the content may have been altered or misleadingly presented. Further verification may be required.

RealityCheck AI does not guarantee truth. It shows you the evidence it can find and how confident that evidence is.
